Our story

A home away from home

Established in 2020, Country Bistro is a premier dining establishment redefining the countryside experience right in the heart of Juja. We are a fusion of modern sophistication and rustic aesthetics — warm lighting, generous portions, and genuine hospitality.

Our carefully curated menu brings together flavours that tantalize every palate, complemented by an extensive beverage selection ranging from fine wines to signature cocktails crafted by our expert bar team.
